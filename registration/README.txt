Registration document -- English translation
============================================

REGISTRATION_rev23_en.md is an English translation of the study's registration
document, revision 23 (2026-10-06). The registration was kept as an online
document in Korean; sections 1-9 were fixed on 2026-10-05 and section 10 is the
dated change log that indexes the amendment, deviation and correction files.

The Korean original is not part of this archive. Its identity at the time of
translation:

  revision   23 (complete export)
  SHA-256    d46e6e2fa30349531964e0cbe5d6d04009845e34589250be714fb237d3263878
             of the UTF-8 XML export of the document body (69,647 bytes)

The translation was prepared for this archive after the study and is not itself
a registered artifact. It is not evidence of when the registration was written:
the hosting service does not provide an independent timestamp that a reader can
check. What a reader can check are the SHA-256 hashes of the amendment files in
study/ (PREREG_AMENDMENTS_MANIFEST.sha256, verified by verify_public.py) and of
the sealed Layer-2 proposals and Layer-4 plans quoted in section 10.

Where the translation and the files in study/ differ, the files in study/ are
authoritative. Population counts in section 1 are as written on 2026-10-05; the
manuscript reports the recomputed counts (study/results/pop_stats.txt).
