## What this actually tells us (more useful than the score)

1. **Layer-declaration revisions are overwhelmingly feature-driven, additive edits.** 9 of 13 are "add a layer
   for a new app/vertical" or "remove a layer for a dropped feature". Only 2 touch the declared bottom
   (kedro: drop `versioning`; legadilo: drop `utils`), and in both the team's move was *remove the layer from the
   contract*, an action our decision table does not have.
2. **This reinterprets the Layer-1 correlation.** S2n and "revision next quarter" are both driven by feature
   activity: busy quarters raise bottom-layer churn AND add new layers. The AUC 0.65 is therefore partly a
   confound, not evidence that teams respond to bottom-layer volatility. This is the strongest reason yet
   that revision is the wrong outcome variable; the pre-registered pivot to change-cost (Layer 4) stands.
3. **The decision table is pointed at the wrong event.** It asks "how should the bottom be restructured";
   teams asked "where does the new thing go". A table that predicted *that* would need a different signal
   (new top-level directories appearing in the window), which is trivial and not architectural.
4. **One genuine hit in direction, not in type**: legadilo `utils` was the most volatile bottom unit for 18
   months (PROMOTE, PROMOTE, EXTRACT proposed); the team eventually decided `utils` is not a layer at all.
   "Remove from contract" should be added to the proposal vocabulary as a fourth type — as a pre-registered
   amendment, applied only to future cases.
5. Two of the 16 "positives" from Layer 1 were not layers-content changes (ignore_imports edits). The Layer-1
   label must be recomputed with the §3 definition; pooled AUC will move slightly.

## Consequences for the study (recorded in pre-reg §10)

- H2 disconfirmed; Layer 2 reported as a negative result with the above analysis.
- Amendment A1 (2026-10-05): add proposal type REMOVE (unit dropped from contract) to §6; applies to new cases only.
- Amendment A2: Layer-1 outcome recomputed as layers-list change only; report both.
- Layer 4 proceeds unchanged — it does not depend on revision labels.
